Electrodynamic & Fluid Boundary Physics in Cryogenic Turbomachinery

Active Magnetic Bearings (AMBs) levitate rocket turbopump shafts rotating beyond $90,000\,\text{RPM}$ without physical contact or grease lubricants, which freeze solid in cryogenic propellants (Liquid Oxygen at $90\,\text{K}$ or Liquid Methane at $111\,\text{K}$). However, sudden fluid-mechanical side-loads caused by main-valve snaps, chugging, or impeller blade-stall create explosive radial forces exceeding $5\,\text{kN}$ in sub-millisecond timescales, threatening catastrophic stator contact.

01. Governing Maxwell Derivation AMB Pole Actuation

Electromagnetic Flux-Force Relation

The radial force $F_{\text{mag}}$ generated by an opposing electromagnet pair operating across a dynamic mechanical airgap $g(t) = g_0 \mp x(t)$ is governed by the electromagnetic field energy in the gap volume:

$$F_{\text{mag}}(\Phi, g) = \frac{\Phi^2}{\mu_0 A_{\text{pole}}} \cos(\theta) - \frac{\Phi_{\text{opp}}^2}{\mu_0 A_{\text{pole}}} \cos(\theta)$$

Where the magnetic flux $\Phi$ is coupled to coil drive current $i(t)$ and pole turns $N$ by reluctance Hopkinson's law:

$$\Phi = \frac{N \cdot i(t)}{\mathcal{R}_{\text{core}} + \frac{2 \cdot g(t)}{\mu_0 A_{\text{pole}}}} \implies F \approx \frac{1}{4} \mu_0 A_{\text{pole}} N^2 \left[ \frac{i_1^2}{(g_0 - x)^2} - \frac{i_2^2}{(g_0 + x)^2} \right]$$
Insight: When gap $g(t) \to 0$, reluctance $\mathcal{R}$ plunges while open-loop magnetic stiffness $k_x = -\frac{\partial F}{\partial x}$ diverges negatively, triggering unrecoverable snap-to-wall contact.
02. Fluid-Structure Interaction Lomakin & Alford Effects

LOX Fluid Annulus Pressure Perturbations

Subcooled dense Liquid Oxygen ($\rho \approx 1141\,\text{kg/m}^3$) under pressures up to $35\,\text{MPa}$ generates intense circumferential pressure gradients when the rotor whirls off-center:

$$\begin{bmatrix} F_{\text{hydro}, x} \\ F_{\text{hydro}, y} \end{bmatrix} = - \begin{bmatrix} K_{xx} & k_{xy} \\ -k_{yx} & K_{yy} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix} C_{xx} & c_{xy} \\ -c_{yx} & C_{yy} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \dot{x} \\ \dot{y} \end{bmatrix}$$

Cross-coupled stiffness $k_{xy} \approx \frac{1}{2} \Omega C_{xx}$ drives destructive self-excited forward subsynchronous whirl when shaft speed $\Omega$ surpasses the critical damping frequency.

$$\nabla p_{\text{cryo}} + \rho (\mathbf{u} \cdot \nabla) \mathbf{u} = -\mu \nabla^2 \mathbf{u} + \mathbf{f}_{\text{vortex}}$$
Lomakin Effect: High pressure drops across impeller seals provide centering stiffness at steady-state, but phase lag during pump start cavitation drops this protection instantly.
03. Real-Time Algorithmic Inversion Kalman-Luenberger Observer

Sensorless Gap Observer via $(F, \Phi)$ Discrepancy

Eddy-current displacement sensors freeze, drift, or exhibit radio noise under cryogenic bath conditions. SmartBearing extracts the precise dynamic gap $g(t)$ directly by fusing high-speed coil search-coil flux $\Phi(t) = \int (V_s - R i) dt$ and commanded force:

$$\hat{g}(t) = \frac{\mu_0 A_{\text{pole}} N \cdot i(t)}{2 \cdot \Phi_{\text{search}}(t)} - \frac{\mu_0 A_{\text{pole}}}{2} \mathcal{R}_{\text{iron}}(\Phi)$$

The Gap Loss Metric $\Delta_{\text{crit}}$ quantifies when $(F, \Phi)$ trace departs from the pre-computed linear safe boundary:

$$\Delta_{\text{crit}}(t) = \left\| \frac{\partial F}{\partial \Phi}\Big|_{\text{meas}} - \frac{2 \Phi_0}{\mu_0 A_{\text{pole}}} \right\| > \epsilon_{\text{threshold}} \implies \text{PULSE}(\mathbf{I}_{\text{emergency}})$$
